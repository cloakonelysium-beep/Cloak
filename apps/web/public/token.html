From the first trade

Private by default

Buy, hold and sell $CLOAK from your private balance at elysium.oncloak.xyz. Each holding sits at a fresh one-time address, funded from Gloam's shielded vault and sent by Cloak's relay, so your wallet never appears.

The goal after that is Gloam's main vault, where $CLOAK becomes a fully shielded asset you can swap, send and pay with privately.

What stays visible is each trade's size on the public pool and the one-time address's balance. Who holds it stays private.

How it gains value

Using Cloak burns $CLOAK

Everything Cloak earns goes to the buyback. That's launches, launch pool trading, private buys, sales and bids, NFT drops, partner integrations and $CLOAK's own pool.

45%buys $CLOAK and burns it for good
15%buys $CLOAK for the ecosystem reserve, like the private pool, grants and drops
40%runs Cloak, from audits to gas to the team
  • $CLOAK's own trading fees go to a contract with no owner. It burns the $CLOAK side at once and sends the HYPE side to the buyback. Nobody can ever redirect them, Cloak included.
  • Buys come in small pieces, close to the average price, at a random time each day, so nobody can trade ahead of them.
  • Bots that rush the opening pay up to 99% in the first 15 minutes, and that goes to the buyback too.
Holders get in first

Your pass to Cloak's auctions

Every week Cloak publishes a holder list from a snapshot at a random block. Every creator who launches a sealed NFT drop on Cloak can offer it to $CLOAK holders with one click.

Cloak's own $CLOAK

Bought in public, locked, never for the team

Cloak buys 2% of the supply in the launch transaction and locks it for 12 months, for the ecosystem only. The team is paid from Cloak's revenue, never from $CLOAK.
