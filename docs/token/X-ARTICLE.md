**Introducing $CLOAK**

Cover image: the Cloak mark on the dark brand background (`apps/web/public/brand`).

---

Money on Elysium is public by default. Every buy, every bid and every balance sits on an explorer for anyone to read.

Cloak changes that. Sealed-bid launches nobody can snipe. Private balances in Gloam's shielded vault. Swaps, sends, payment links and bids that never show your wallet.

Today we're introducing the token that ties it together.

## What $CLOAK is

$CLOAK is the token of Cloak, the privacy layer for Elysium.

1,000,000,000 tokens. Fixed. Nothing can ever mint more.

No team allocation. No presale. No private round. No airdrop farm.

It launches on Montra, paired with HYPE, with the whole supply in one Uniswap v4 position whose liquidity can never be removed.

## Private from the first trade

Most tokens go private months after launch, if ever.

$CLOAK is private from its first trade. Through Cloak's own Montra page at elysium.oncloak.xyz you can buy, hold and sell $CLOAK from your private balance. Each holding sits at a fresh one-time address, funded from Gloam's vault and sent by Cloak's relay, so your wallet never appears.

The goal after that is Gloam's main vault, where $CLOAK becomes a fully shielded asset you can swap, send and pay with privately like HYPE and USDC.

## Value from use, not from holding

$CLOAK pays no yield and no share of revenue. Instead, using Cloak buys it and burns it.

Everything Cloak earns goes to the buyback. That's the fee on every successful launch on Cloak, a share of every launch pool's trading, our part of every private buy, sale and bid, NFT drops, partner integrations, and $CLOAK's own pool.

60% of it buys $CLOAK in the market, and 75% of what it buys is burned for good. That's 45% of everything Cloak earns, turned into burned $CLOAK. The rest runs Cloak and builds the ecosystem.

Buys come in small pieces, close to the average price, at a random time each day, so nobody can trade ahead of them. Every buy and every burn is on chain and listed on the token page.

More launches, more private trading and more drops mean more $CLOAK burned.

## Where the fees go

$CLOAK's trading fees go to a contract with no owner. It burns the $CLOAK side straight away and sends the HYPE side to the buyback. On Montra only the current fee recipient can change where fees go, and this contract has no way to do it. Nobody can ever redirect $CLOAK's fees. Not even us.

Bots that rush the opening pay up to 99% in the first 15 minutes, and that goes to the buyback too.

## Holders get in first

$CLOAK is your pass to Cloak's auctions.

Every week Cloak publishes a holder list.

Hold 1,000,000 $CLOAK and you get guaranteed spots in NFT drops on Cloak, minted at a fixed price before bidding opens.

Hold 100,000 $CLOAK and you get a boost in every sealed auction. Your bids rank higher and you pay up to 23% less than everyone else.

Any creator who launches a drop on Cloak can offer this to $CLOAK holders with one click. Cloak's own genesis drop comes in launch week.

Private holders count too. Holdings at Cloak's one-time addresses are on the list, and they can use their spots without showing their main wallet.

## Holders decide

From day 7, $CLOAK holders vote. The first vote decides when $CLOAK's own trading fee comes down. After that, holders decide on drop terms, which launchpads Cloak brings into its private vaults next, and how the ecosystem reserve is used.

Private holders vote from their one-time address, so their wallet stays out of it.

## Cloak's own $CLOAK

Cloak buys 2% of the supply in the launch transaction, in public, and locks it for 12 months. It's only for the ecosystem, like the private pool, grants and the genesis drop. Never for the team. The team is paid from Cloak's revenue, never from $CLOAK.

## The roadmap

Pay your launch fee in $CLOAK. Launch with 1% of your raise in $CLOAK, bought and burned, instead of 1.5% in HYPE.

Premium sealed-bid launches for holders. Holder bids rank higher and pay less in token launches, and creators can reserve part of a sale for a holders-only round.

Private holder lists. Prove you hold enough $CLOAK with a zero-knowledge proof instead of a public snapshot.

Private voting. The same holder vote, with zero-knowledge proofs.

## What $CLOAK will never do

No yield. No staking rewards. No share of revenue. $CLOAK is not a share in Cloak.

No minting. No transfer tax. No owner. No upgradeable buyback.

No perk that asks you to show your wallet.

## The honest part

Burns follow use. If Cloak is busy, more $CLOAK is burned. If it's quiet, less is.

Trades in the public pool show their size, even when the trader is private.

None of this is a promise about price. It's how Cloak uses what it earns, written in contracts you can check.
